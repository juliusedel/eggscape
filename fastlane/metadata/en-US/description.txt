The egg-and-spoon race from every kids' party, now with your phone: your iPhone is the spoon with an egg on it. Tilt it gently to keep the egg in the middle while you run. Tilt too far and it rolls over the rim and breaks.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold your phone flat in front of you like a spoon and calibrate your grip.
• Start running: every step makes the egg wobble.
• Hold “Sprint” to go faster, but the egg wobbles more.
• Feel the buzz? The closer the egg gets to the rim, the stronger your phone vibrates.

REALISTIC
• A real stainless-steel spoon and a photo-like egg, seen from above.
• Pick a white background or camera mode, where you look through your phone at the ground you are running on.

GAME MODES
• Time trials over 15, 50 or 100 meters, with personal bests.
• Endless: run as far as you can. The weather gets rougher every 100 meters.
• Pass the phone: 2 to 4 players run the same course one after another.
• Nearby: up to 4 phones in the same room, no internet needed.

WEATHER
Wind pushes the egg sideways, rain makes the spoon slippery and splashes through puddles, and in a thunderstorm every lightning bolt makes you jump.

EGG SHOP
Earn coins while running and spend them on dyed, dotted, striped, pearl or golden eggs. If you don't want to wait, you can buy coin packs.

LEADERBOARDS
Compare your best times with the world and your friends through Game Center.

No ads, no tracking, no sign-up required.
