The classic egg-and-spoon race, now on your phone: your iPhone is the spoon, with an egg on it. Tilt it gently to keep the egg in the middle. Tilt too far and it rolls over the rim and breaks.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold your phone flat in front of you like a spoon and calibrate your grip.
• The course runs by itself. Every step makes the egg wobble, and small tilts keep it in the middle.
• Hold “Sprint” to go faster, but the egg wobbles more.
• Feel the buzz? The closer the egg gets to the rim, the stronger your phone vibrates.
• You can play standing still. If you walk or run along anyway, keep an eye on your surroundings.

THE LOOK
• A spoon that looks like stainless steel and a photo-like egg, seen from above.
• By default you see the live camera picture behind the spoon, as if you were looking through your phone at the ground. The picture is only shown, never saved or sent.
• Prefer no camera? Then you play on a white background.

GAME MODES
• Time trials over 15, 50 or 100 meters, with personal bests. You get three eggs per run.
• Endless: how far can you get? The weather gets rougher every 100 meters.
• Pass the Phone: 2 to 4 players run the same course one after another on one phone.
• Nearby: up to 4 phones in the same room, no internet needed.
• Online: 2 to 4 players over Game Center. Invite friends or let Game Center find players for you.

WEATHER
Wind pushes the egg sideways, rain makes the spoon slippery and splashes through puddles, and in a thunderstorm every lightning strike jolts the egg. Before each time trial you pick the weather or leave it to chance.

EGG SHOP
Earn coins in single player and in nearby or online races, and unlock 19 more eggs: from a brown farm egg to dyed, dotted and striped ones, up to marble, pearl, silver and gold. If you don't want to wait, you can buy coin packs.

LEADERBOARDS
Compare your best times and your endless record with the world and your friends through Game Center.

No ads, no tracking, no account needed. Only online races and leaderboards need Game Center.
