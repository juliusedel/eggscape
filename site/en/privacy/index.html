Privacy Policy

For the iPhone app Eggscape and this website. Last updated: November 2026

The short version

  • Eggscape has no servers of its own and no user accounts. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics tools and no built-in software from other companies.
  • Your settings, your player name, your best times, coins and eggs stay on your iPhone. I have no access to them.
  • The camera picture is only shown on your screen. It is never recorded, stored or sent.
  • When you play with others, their phones receive your player name (online, your Game Center name), your egg and the race data.
  • Online races, leaderboards and purchases run through Apple.

2. Data on your iPhone

Eggscape stores the following in the app’s own storage on your iPhone:

  • your settings: sound, vibration, sensitivity, background, your calibration and whether you have seen the tutorial,
  • your player name,
  • your best times and your endless record,
  • your coins, the eggs you have unlocked and the egg you play with,
  • the numbers of the purchases whose coins have already been credited, so that no purchase counts twice,
  • for Nearby, one randomly generated identifier (peer ID) per player name, which iOS needs for connections to phones nearby.

This data only leaves your iPhone in the cases described in sections 5 and 6. I have no access to it. Like all app data, it is included in your iPhone’s backups if you make any.

You can delete it yourself at any time: in Eggscape under Settings → “Reset Data” (this also deletes purchased coins), or by deleting the app.

3. Camera

By default, Eggscape shows the live picture of your rear camera behind the spoon, as if you could see through your phone. For this, iOS asks on your first run whether Eggscape may use the camera.

The picture is only shown on your screen, and only during a run. Eggscape records nothing, stores nothing, analyzes nothing and sends nothing. Without permission you play on a white background. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the iOS Settings under Eggscape, or choose “White” in the app under Settings → Background.

4. Motion sensors

Only during a run and while calibrating, including in the room before a multiplayer race, Eggscape reads your iPhone’s tilt from the accelerometer and gyroscope to steer the egg. These values are only processed on your iPhone and never leave it. The only thing stored is the pose you set as level when you calibrate.

5. Nearby multiplayer

When you create or join a room under Multiplayer → Nearby, iOS asks whether Eggscape may use your local network. The phones then connect directly to each other over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity). This needs neither the internet nor a server, and the connection is encrypted.

  • While your room is open, Eggscape players nearby who are looking for a room see your player name.
  • The phones in your room receive your player name, a randomly generated identifier, your egg and the race data: whether you are ready, distance and weather, how far you have got, how many eggs you have left, your time and the result.
  • Eggscape only shows this data on the other phones in the room and during the race, and does not store it there.

You can withdraw access to the local network at any time in the iOS Settings under Eggscape. Nearby then stops working; everything else keeps working.

6. Online races and leaderboards (Game Center)

For online races and leaderboards, Eggscape uses Apple’s Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, Eggscape signs you in there when the app starts.

  • Leaderboards: after single-player time trials that you finish and after endless runs, Eggscape sends your result to Game Center. There, other Game Center players see your best result together with your Game Center name.
  • Online races: under Multiplayer → Online, Game Center finds players for you, or you invite friends. During the race, your Game Center name, your Game Center player ID, the race data as in section 5 and, in some cases, your egg go to the other players through Game Center.

Apple processes this data under its own responsibility; for users in the EU, that is Apple Distribution International Ltd. in Ireland. You can read more in Apple’s Privacy Policy and under Game Center & Privacy. If you sign out of Game Center in the iOS Settings, there are no online races and leaderboards; everything else keeps working.

7. Purchases

You buy coin packs from Apple in the App Store. Apple is the seller, handles the payment and processes your payment data under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Eggscape receives a signed confirmation of the purchase from Apple and only uses what it needs to credit your coins: which pack you bought and the purchase number. I receive sales reports from Apple that contain no information about you as a person.

8. Statistics and crash reports from Apple

If you have agreed in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → “Share With App Developers”), Apple provides me with crash reports and usage statistics for Eggscape. Apple only shares them in aggregated form or in a form that does not identify you. Eggscape itself collects nothing for this. You can read more under App Analytics & Privacy.

9. Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your email address and your message in order to reply. I delete the email once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention periods require me to keep it longer.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the USA. When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, even if you have no GitHub account. I do not get to see this data. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has found an adequate level of data protection. You can read more in GitHub’s privacy statement.

The website sets no cookies, loads no fonts, scripts or content from other servers and uses no analytics or tracking services.

11. Legal bases

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: for the game features you use (multiplayer, leaderboards and purchases) and for replies to requests about a purchase.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: for all other requests and for running this website securely. My legitimate interest is to answer questions and to offer the website reliably and securely.
  • Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG: Eggscape only stores data on your iPhone as far as this is strictly necessary for the features you want to use.
  • Eggscape only uses the camera and the local network if you have allowed it in iOS. You can withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS Settings.

You don’t have to give Eggscape any data: without a player name you play as “Player”, without the camera you play on white, and without Game Center only online races and leaderboards are missing.

12. How long data is kept

  • Data on your iPhone: until you reset it or delete the app.
  • Data on your fellow players’ phones: only while the room and the race last.
  • Emails: until your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention periods apply.
  • Data at Apple and GitHub: according to their privacy policies.

13. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). Just send me an email. As I hold no data about you apart from your emails, this usually only concerns those. You can view, change and delete the data on your iPhone yourself. For data held by Apple, for example about Game Center or purchases, please contact Apple, for example through privacy.apple.com.

Right to object: Where I process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place (Art. 77 GDPR).

14. No automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making and no profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

15. Children

Eggscape collects no data from anyone, including children. With Screen Time in iOS, parents can restrict purchases and Game Center and use Ask to Buy to approve every purchase their child makes. As a player name, a nickname is better than your full name, because players nearby see it.

16. Changes

I update this privacy policy when the app, this website or the law changes. The version published here applies.
