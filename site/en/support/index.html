Frequently asked questions

Do I have to run while playing?

No. The course runs by itself. You hold your phone like a spoon and tilt it to keep the egg in the middle, and you can do that standing still. If you walk or run along anyway, only play where there is enough room and keep an eye on your surroundings.

The egg reacts too strongly or too weakly.

Before the start, hold your phone the way you want to play and tap “Calibrate”: that pose then counts as level. In Settings (the gear at the top right) you can adjust the sensitivity and recalibrate at any time.

Why does Eggscape ask for the camera?

By default, Eggscape shows the live picture of your rear camera behind the spoon, as if you could see through your phone. The picture is only shown, never recorded, stored or sent. Without the camera you play on a white background; you can switch in Settings under “Background”. If you declined the camera once and want to use it after all, allow it in the iOS Settings under Eggscape.

Nearby doesn’t find a room.

  • On one phone, someone taps “Create Room” under Multiplayer → Nearby; on the other phones, tap “Join”. A room has space for up to 4 phones.
  • Keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on and stay close to the phone with the room. You don’t need the internet.
  • Allow Eggscape to access the local network on every phone: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • Update Eggscape on every phone. Phones whose versions are too far apart don’t see each other’s rooms.

How do I play online?

You need the internet and must be signed in to Game Center on your iPhone (iOS Settings → Game Center). In Eggscape, tap “Find Players” under Multiplayer → Online. Then invite friends or let Game Center find players for you. A race has 2 to 4 players.

How does Pass the Phone work?

2 to 4 players run the same course with the same weather one after another and pass the phone on. At the end, the standings show who was fastest. Because everyone shares one phone, these runs don’t count for best times, leaderboards or coins.

How do I get coins and new eggs?

You earn coins in single player and in nearby or online races: the further, faster and stormier, the more. Use them to unlock new eggs in the Shop under “Eggs”. Under “Coins” you can also buy coin packs.

My coins, eggs or best times are gone.

They are only stored on your iPhone, not in a cloud. “Reset Data” in Settings and deleting the app remove them, including purchased coins. They only move to a new iPhone if you restore a backup of your old iPhone there. Your results on the Game Center leaderboards are kept.

Can I restore purchases?

Coin packs are consumables, and according to Apple such purchases cannot be restored. That is why Eggscape has no restore button.

A purchase didn’t arrive, or I want a refund.

Purchases are handled by Apple. If a purchase is still waiting for approval, for example through Ask to Buy in Family Sharing, the coins arrive as soon as it is approved. If coins are missing after a purchase, close Eggscape completely and open it again: the app credits open purchases when it starts. You can request a refund from Apple.

How do I change my player name?

In Settings under “Player Name”. Others see it when you play nearby or on one phone together. Online and on the leaderboards, your Game Center name appears.

How do I delete my data?

In Eggscape under Settings → “Reset Data”, or delete the app. You manage data held by Apple, such as your Game Center results, through privacy.apple.com. You can read more in the privacy policy.
